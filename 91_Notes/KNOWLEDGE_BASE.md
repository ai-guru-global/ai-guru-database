## 🌟 Why AI Guru?

### The Problem

The AI landscape evolves faster than ever. With new models, frameworks, and techniques emerging daily, learners struggle with:
- **Fragmented information** scattered across blogs, papers, and videos
- **Outdated content** that doesn't reflect 2024-2026 advancements
- **Gap between theory and practice** - knowing transformers vs. deploying RAG systems
- **No clear learning path** from beginner to production engineer

### The Solution

**AI Guru** is a **production-grade knowledge base** that bridges theory and practice:

| Feature | What You Get |
|---------|-------------|
| 🎯 **Dual Learning Paths** | 速成路径 (9 guides) for quick onboarding + 系统学习 (13 chapters) for deep mastery |
| 🆕 **2026 Latest** | 12 in-depth reports on cutting-edge topics: GPT-5.2, Claude 4.5, VLA robotics, World Models |
| 🎓 **University-Grade** | Complete AI general education curriculum with 10 textbooks, 6 case studies, 8 labs |
| 🏭 **Production-Ready** | Real-world deployment guides: K8s, MLOps, Agent infrastructure, cost optimization |
| 🔍 **Full-Text Search** | Modern web frontend with instant search across 2.6M+ characters |
| 🌐 **Bilingual** | Chinese primary, English code/concepts - accessible to 1.4B Chinese speakers |
